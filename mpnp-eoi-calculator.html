Notes on this calculator's scoring logic
  • Factor 1 (Language): First and Second Official Language are scored separately and add together (max 50 from the inputs, though the source labels the factor max as 125).
  • Factor 3 (Work): Years of experience and "fully recognized by a provincial licensing body" stack (max 75 + 100 = 175).
  • Factor 5 (Adaptability): All nine options share one selection group — only one can be chosen (matching the source site; the sub-categories do not sum).
  • Factor 6 (Risk Assessment): Checkboxes — multiple can be selected and they subtract (down to −200).
